Privacy Policy for Tiktok Shop Vids

Effective Date: November 23, 2026

1. Overview

Tiktok Shop Vids ("the Application") respects user privacy and operates strictly as a personal publishing bridge between the owner's workflow tools and their authorized TikTok account.

2. Information Accessed

When authorized via TikTok OAuth, the Application accesses only the specific permissions required to function:

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic) to verify the connected TikTok account.
  • Video upload and publishing permissions (video.upload, video.publish) to send showcase videos to the owner's TikTok drafts or profile.

3. Data Storage & Sharing

  • No Third-Party Data Collection: This Application does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data from external users or TikTok viewers.
  • Credentials & Tokens: OAuth access tokens are handled securely through the owner's private integration environment solely to authenticate API requests to TikTok and are never shared with third parties.

4. Data Retention & Revocation

Access to the connected TikTok account can be revoked at any time directly through the TikTok app under Settings and privacy > Security and permissions > Apps and services, which immediately terminates all API access.
